MP4DX - Last 5 Turns Duels: During the Last 5 Turns, a player who ends their move on a space where a rival stands challenges them to a Duel, hosted by a Bob-omb with the Battle Space music. The challenger bets Coins (10, 20, half or all of the rival's coins; whoever has less bets everything they have), 1 Star for 40 coins (challenger's 40 coins against the rival's Star) or 1 Star each, then picks one of two random 1-vs-1 mini-games (Bowser Bop, Mystic Match 'Em, Archaeologuess, Goomba's Chip Flip, Kareening Koopas, Bowser Wrestling, Panels of Doom). The winner takes everything; a tie refunds the bets. CPU vs CPU duels are simulated when "show COM mini-games" is off. After the duel, the space's own event runs. The board hosts announce the new rule at the end of the Last Five Turns Event.
The duel code lives in the 'bonus stars + duels' ISO (DX code list in main.dol, texts in mess/board_e.dat banks 57-58). This code is only the on/off switch; with it off, or on any other ISO, nothing changes. Only for MP4 DX (GMPE01).
